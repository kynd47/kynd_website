OUR STORY

Three friends, one little idea.

Kynd began with three best friends who simply wanted to make something together.

We started with paint, fabric and a lot of ideas. What began as something we made for fun slowly became something other people wanted to carry too.

Today, we create hand-painted totes and pouches from our little corner of Cuttack, Odisha. Every piece starts with an idea and ends with a brush in someone's hand.

— the people behind kynd

HOW WE WORK
